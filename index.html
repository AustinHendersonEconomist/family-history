How to read the maps

Press Play. The map pauses at each ★ story until you press Continue, or untick “Pause for ★ stories” to let it run. In the Chronicle, record opens the document itself, and tree or page opens a profile. Thick lines rest on a record, thin lines on family trees or family memory, dashed lines are inferred, and dotted lines are doubtful.

Living people are left out. The research was done by Austin in November 2026 with Claude, an AI model, and will have mistakes. Corrections are welcome. One has already fixed this site.
