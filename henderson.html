About the Haiti thread

A single note on FamilySearch says Nancy Manack's father, "French John" Manack, came "from San Domingo". In the 1700s that usually meant Saint-Domingue, the French colony that became Haiti. No source is given, and it could also mean Spanish Santo Domingo.

The map shows it as a dotted line because it is unproven. Elbert County deed records, or his 1796 estate file, could confirm where he came from.

About this map

Thanks to a correction from a family member, later moves now follow the family’s own memory. Lore vs. records lists where the documents and the stories disagree.

This map was researched by Austin in November 2026, with Claude (an AI model), using census and vital records on FamilySearch, an Ancestry family tree, Georgia probate records, Texas State Library service records, published county and town histories, and sourced profiles on WikiTree for the New England families. A few links in those early lines still rest on family trees.

Thick lines are journeys backed by a record. Thin lines come from family trees that haven't been checked, dashed lines are reasonable guesses (for example, an emigration date worked out from where children were born), and dotted lines are family stories that remain unproven.

★ stories set the family against the history happening around them. Turn off “Pause for ★ stories” to let the map run straight through.
